# ยานพาหนะ (fleet) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A live "ยานพาหนะ" module. บุคลากร file a ใบขอใช้รถ, their head agrees through the central workflow, and the หัวหน้าหมวดยานพาหนะ puts it on a เที่ยวรถ (car + พนักงานขับรถ + time range) from a dashboard. Drivers log ออกเดินทาง and กลับถึง with odometer readings.

**Architecture:**
- A ใบขอใช้รถ is a `fleet_request` row. A normal one owns a central **คำขอ** (`moduleCode: 'fleet'`, target `NEAREST_HEAD`); one filed from an approved คำขอไปราชการ has none.
- The displayed status is derived by a pure function from the head คำขอ status, `dispatch_status` and the เที่ยวรถ status (spec "Status of a ใบขอใช้รถ").
- `FleetRequestService` (file, resubmit, cancel), `FleetDispatchService` (assign, move, decline, trip edits, log) and `FleetCatalogService` (cars, drivers) write; `FleetQueries` reads. Car/driver overlap is checked after locking the car and driver rows.
- One optional seam in `ApprovalService` tells fleet managers when a head agrees.
- Next.js routes and pages under `/fleet` and `/api/fleet`.

**Tech Stack:** pnpm monorepo, TypeScript, zod 4, mysql2 on MariaDB 11.8, Drizzle schema, Next.js App Router, Vitest, Testing Library, Playwright (screenshots).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-27-fleet-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- **Migration:** `packages/db/migrations/0015_fleet.sql`, additive, listed after 0014 in `migrate.ts`. Drizzle in `packages/db/src/schema/fleet.ts`, exported from the schema index. New tables added to `infra/backup/restore-verify.test.sh`. Row 0015 added to `docs/integration/modules.md`.
- **Module code:** `FLEET_MODULE = 'fleet'` in `packages/core/src/fleet/fleet-module.ts`. Routes `/fleet/...`, `/api/fleet/...`.
- **Enums:** vehicle kind `SEDAN` เก๋ง, `PICKUP` กระบะ, `VAN` ตู้, `AMBULANCE` รถพยาบาล, `OTHER` อื่น ๆ; vehicle status `ACTIVE` ใช้งาน, `IN_REPAIR` ซ่อม, `RETIRED` ปลดระวาง; dispatch `WAITING`, `ASSIGNED`, `DECLINED`, `CANCELLED`; trip `PLANNED`, `DEPARTED`, `RETURNED`, `CANCELLED`.
- **Displayed status codes** (`FleetRequestStatus`): `HEAD_REVIEW` รอหัวหน้าเห็นชอบ, `RETURNED` ส่งกลับแก้ไข, `HEAD_REJECTED` ไม่เห็นชอบ, `WAITING` รอจัดรถ, `ASSIGNED` จัดรถแล้ว, `DEPARTED` ออกเดินทาง, `DONE` เสร็จสิ้น, `DECLINED` ไม่จัดรถ, `CANCELLED` ยกเลิก.
- **Time rules:** `depart_at < return_at`; `depart_at >= now`; Bangkok date of `depart_at` ≤ Bangkok today + 90; `return_at - depart_at` ≤ 30 days; both on 15-minute steps (minutes % 15 === 0, seconds 0). Stored UTC.
- **Odometer:** integer 0–9,999,999. Departure ≥ the car's last `odometer_in` (any value if none). Return ≥ `odometer_out`. Departure allowed from `starts_at - 2h`.
- **Reference:** `VR-<พ.ศ. of fiscal year>-<4-digit sequence>`, sequence per fiscal year in `fleet_request_sequence (fiscal_year INT PK, last_value INT)` locked `FOR UPDATE`, as room booking does.
- **Permission:** `fleet.manage`, role `fleet-manager`, not data-owner gated. Check is `authorizer.organizationScope(subject, 'fleet.manage') === 'all'` wrapped as `canManageFleet(subject)`. A driver is a person with `fleet_driver.is_active = 1`.
- **Audit actions:** `fleet.request.submitted`, `.resubmitted`, `.cancelled`, `.assigned`, `.unassigned`, `.declined`; `fleet.trip.updated`, `.departed`, `.returned`, `.cancelled`; `fleet.vehicle.create`, `.update`; `fleet.driver.create`, `.update`. Metadata: reference, ids, statuses, odometer only. Never purpose, destination, passenger names or reasons. Each action gets a Thai label on the audit page.
- **Notifications:** `notification.in_app`, `moduleCode: 'fleet'`, idempotency `notification.in_app:fleet:<event>:<id>:<recipient>`, subjects exactly as the spec's Notifications table. Head-stage notices route through `notificationTarget('fleet', requestId)` → `/fleet/requests/by-request/<requestId>`, a page that redirects to `/fleet/requests/<fleetRequestId>` (same pattern as travel).
- **Error codes** (`FleetErrorCode`): `INVALID_RANGE`, `IN_PAST`, `TOO_FAR_AHEAD`, `TOO_LONG`, `NOT_QUARTER_HOUR`, `NO_ORGANIZATION`, `NO_APPROVER`, `TRAVEL_NOT_ELIGIBLE`, `TRAVEL_ALREADY_HAS_CAR`, `NOT_FOUND`, `NOT_PENDING`, `NOT_RETURNED`, `VEHICLE_BUSY`, `DRIVER_BUSY`, `VEHICLE_UNAVAILABLE`, `DRIVER_INACTIVE`, `TRIP_DOES_NOT_COVER`, `DEPARTURE_TOO_EARLY`, `ODOMETER_BELOW_LAST`, `ODOMETER_BELOW_OUT`, `DRIVER_HAS_TRIPS`, `DUPLICATE_REGISTRATION`.
- **UI:** Thai copy in `packages/i18n/src/th.ts` with CONTEXT.md terms; accent `#a63d40`; confirm/reason steps use `ActionDialog`; DESIGN.md components.

## Review Focus

1. **Two dispatchers, one car:** two concurrent assignments of the same car (or driver) over overlapping times: exactly one succeeds, the other gets 409 `VEHICLE_BUSY` / `DRIVER_BUSY` (Task 6).
2. **Bangkok day edge:** at 23:30 Bangkok on day D, a ใบ departing on D+90 at 08:00 is accepted and D+91 is refused; `now` is injected (Task 2).
3. **Late head approval:** a head agrees after `depart_at` has passed; the ใบ still appears in รอจัดรถ, flagged overdue, and can be assigned or declined (Tasks 6, 7).
4. **Cancel after departure:** the requester cancelling a ใบ whose เที่ยวรถ is DEPARTED gets `NOT_PENDING`; cancelling the last ใบ on a PLANNED เที่ยวรถ cancels it and tells the driver (Task 5).
5. **Generic request routes:** `POST /api/requests`, resubmission and cancellation refuse a `fleet` คำขอ with `fleet_request_route` (Task 8).
